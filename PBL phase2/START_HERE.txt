GRAPH BASED FRIEND AND OPPORTUNITY RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM  (Neo4j + C++)
=======================================================================

STEPS (double-click each file, in this order):

 0. Open Neo4j Desktop and make sure your database says RUNNING.
 1. Double-click  1_build.bat      -> makes app.exe and load.exe
 2. Double-click  2_load_data.bat  -> puts 100 users into Neo4j (first time only)
 3. Double-click  3_run_app.bat    -> starts the program
